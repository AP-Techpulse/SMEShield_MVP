## 1. Executive Summary of Critical Alignments

1. **Approved Objectives Count:** Earlier working documents summarized project objectives under 5 general headings. The official approved dissertation specification (PRD v2.0) defines **Seven Approved Objectives**. The dissertation must restore and rigorously structure Chapters 1, 4, and 5 around all 7 objectives.
2. **Adaptive Engine Mechanism:** The adaptation operates on **curriculum module recommendation sequencing** (prioritizing modules by SME sector and diagnostic weakness), **not** dynamic intra-quiz question branch logic. The quizzes maintain a standardized 5-question baseline per module for consistent psychometric evaluation.
3. **Machine Learning Claims Elimination:** The adaptive engine is **strictly deterministic and rule-based**. Any legacy text suggesting AI, machine learning models, or neural networks must be corrected to prevent academic penalization during the external defence.
4. **Awareness Label Terminology:** The highest awareness tier in the Human Firewall formulation is **"Highly Aware"** ($\ge 85$), not the deprecated term **"Expert"** (which implied professional security certification).
5. **Construct Distinction (Baseline vs. HFS):** The difference between Baseline Diagnostic Score and HFS ($\Delta_{\text{Awareness}}$) is an internal training progression metric, not a clinically proven causal guarantee of breach immunity.
6. **Administrative Portal Scope:** The planned *SMEShield Admin Portal* is supporting future infrastructure, not a fourth assessed learner role in this study.
